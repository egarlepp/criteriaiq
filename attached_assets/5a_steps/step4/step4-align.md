# 🔤 Step4 - Align

## Align (Technical Clearance & Ongoing Engagement)

**Goal:** Validate Technical Validation success, secure Technical Win (TCC), and drive relationship building\n**Responsibilities:**

* Lead weekly syncs with customer
* Track progress against MAP and Execution Criteria
* Conduct final validation + exec readout
* Declare Technical Win (TCC) when all criteria are met
* Prepare full handoff to PSO:
  * Completed Artifacts: MAP, Design Docs, Transition Plan


**ALIGNing** on technical and business objectives becomes essential as the opportunity moves toward a decision. Here, the Sales Engineer (SE) seeks to achieve **Technical Clearance to Close (TCC)** — customer acknowledgment that the evaluated solution meets all critical requirements and is their chosen path forward.

* **Validation:** Double-check that the solution design meets every functional, performance, and integration requirement outlined in the earlier stages.
* **Team Synchronization:** Ensure all relevant stakeholders — sales reps, product teams, Proserv, support, and executive sponsors — are fully informed about the technical approval and any remaining tasks required during hand-off.
* **Roadmap & Next Steps:** Include final timelines, implementation details, and potential adoption challenges. Secure sign-offs from the right people to confirm the solution is technically approved.
* **Post-TCC Engagement:** Even after receiving a technical win, the SE remains engaged during commercial validation, legal review, and procurement. Ongoing involvement ensures **alignment** between the technical, financial, and operational teams and helps address last-minute questions or obstacles.

By attaining TCC and maintaining a visible presence through the final procurement steps, you reinforce trust, prevent misunderstandings, and help guarantee a smooth transition from technical approval to contract signature. Ultimately, this collaborative approach drives the deal to a successful close while preserving valuable relationships for future opportunities.


:::info
**Technically Cleared to Close(TCC)** is the acknowledgment from a prospect that Internal is the chosen technology for the project our software is currently being assessed to address. There must be NO technical hurdles that would jeopardize the oppty of not being won.

**It is represented by a check box in the TAP.**

:::



**EX 1** - Customer has finished trialing Internal, but now needs to do their due diligence by testing Varonis

* In this scenario the opportunity is not yet TCC
* SE should re-confirm key points of unique value with the customer, and arrange a follow up technical call post customer evaluation with Varonis to gain feedback on how we compared
* If following that call the customer confirmed that Ivanti is the chosen technical solution then the opportunity can then be marked as TCC

**EX 2** - Customer has trialed Internal and 2 competitors, and confirmed that Internal is ahead technically but the decision will still be impacted by final pricing review

* In this case the opportunity can be marked as TCC with agreement from opportunity owner (AD/SE/SE Director) that the customer has recognized technical/ business value over and above the competition
* This value then be used by the opportunity owner to assist in the closing process.

**EX 3** - Customer has trialed Internal and competing solution and stated that both are neck and neck technically, and the decision will come down to price

* In this scenario as there is no confirmation from the customer the opportunity cannot be marked as TCC until the final decision is given
* The SE should work with the customer to demonstrate value over and above the competition
* Only if the customer then agrees we are ahead technically, and the opportunity owner concurs (AE, AD, SE Leader) can the opportunity then be marked as TCC.
