## Affect (Shaping the Opportunity Through Technical Validation)

**Goal:** Architect, deploy, and execute the technical validation\n**Responsibilities:**

* Design POC architecture (CloudLAB or Onsite)
* Build and test environment (install/configure/validate)
* Document technical implementation (Requirements & Design Docs)
* Run POC with customer (validate use cases)
* Collaborate with Product/Engineering to:
  * Enable advanced features or flags - Product Gaps?
  * Resolve blockers
  * Log feedback


Armed with the knowledge from the Assess stage, you're now poised to influence the direction of the opportunity and **AFFECT** its momentum. This is where you propose well-aligned solutions, demonstrate your expertise, and illustrate the tangible business impact of your offering. At the same time, you'll drive the deal forward by conducting **technical validation activities** — such as demos, [Take Our Software for a Spin](/doc/fbbc75c4-3a03-4ca5-b12f-2821ebfd8cb3)  exercises, [Playbook - Wellness Workshop | HealthCheck](/doc/af1e107d-6372-44bd-bcd8-e92350ad1e61) , etc.— to showcase real-world use cases, engage key stakeholders, and highlight both business and technical value.

* **Map Each Feature to the Need:** Show precisely how your product addresses the specific gaps identified during the Assess stage. This clarity ensures stakeholders can easily link your solution's capabilities to their most pressing problems.
* **Quantify the Benefits:** Highlight measurable outcomes like time saved or cost reductions, as concrete data resonates far more than broad statements. This helps stakeholders justify investment and see the value in real terms.
* **Craft a Persuasive Narrative:** Use customer-focused language and share relevant success stories. By demonstrating how similar organizations have benefited, you reinforce the credibility of your solution and boost customer confidence.

Throughout this process, capture all technical validation activities, outcomes, and stakeholder feedback in your [Technical Account Plan(TAP) Review](/doc/eb71f1e8-c7d1-4dcf-b15d-c610d0c90ce4) . Maintaining detailed records promotes transparency, reveals new coaching or optimization opportunities, and keeps everyone aligned. By documenting these key insights, you reinforce customer trust, maintain a clear roadmap to closure, and position yourself for a smooth and decisive technical sign-off.
