# 🔤 Step2 - Assess

## Assess (Technical Account Plan Ownership)

**Goal:** Gather deep technical understanding and scope the technical validation process\n**Responsibilities:**

* Research customer's environment, tools, industry - [Effective Preparation](/doc/b85dc5e4-eeee-452d-9542-6bec9b43f207) 
* Review, if applicable,  any RFx requests - [Playbook - RFx Request](/doc/e8f9fdd8-d2b4-4856-bb69-6f3e49cc2dff) 
* Identify key technical influencers and decision-makers
* Conduct discovery sessions to capture:
  * Use cases
  * Technical requirements
  * Security & compliance considerations
* Draft Initial **Artifacts** - [POC | POV Playbook](/doc/5844508c-a599-4e90-8f7f-a55d08b2ec7d) :
  * MAP (Mutual Action Plan)
  * Execution Criteria Plan
  * Security and Compliance Docs

    \

At this stage, the Sales Engineer (SE) creates and maintains a [Technical Account Plan(TAP) Review](/doc/eb71f1e8-c7d1-4dcf-b15d-c610d0c90ce4) , dynamically documenting and assuring every crucial detail of the opportunity's technical landscape. The TAP evolves throughout the deal cycle and serves as all stakeholders' central source of truth. **Presently**, TAP is maintained on the EVALUATION record and connected to the Opportunity.

**Key Information to Capture**

* Current situation and next steps
* Technical champions and key contacts
* SE notes, statuses, and milestones
* Validation activities
* Additional management feedback improving forecasting efficiency
* Extensive Information on the fields and requirements - [Evaluations (POC|POV)](/doc/5844508c-a599-4e90-8f7f-a55d08b2ec7d) 

This tactical blueprint informs analytics, coaching opportunities, root-cause analysis, and in-depth discussions. By owning and regularly updating the TAP, the SE can:

* **Anticipate Challenges:** Identify potential roadblocks early and plan strategies to address them.
* **Guide Technical Validation:** Align with stakeholders on test plans, proofs of concept, or demos to confirm solution feasibility.
* **Drive Technical Clearance to Closure (TCC):** Keep teams coordinated on essential steps needed to secure a technical win.

Ultimately, consistent, data-driven evaluations help ensure that specialists, management, and the extended sales team remain on the same page, fostering a smooth path to success as the opportunity progresses.

**Supporting / Specialist SEs - Update __TAP__ and the Current Situation based on your support if needed. Be sure to update that field with the Date and Initials.**


# **What is TAP?**

# **A Technical Plan owned by the Assigned SE and updated by ALL throughout the opportunity's lifecycle until closure. Along with entering supporting activities, a tactical blueprint of the opportunity is developed, providing analytics, coaching opportunities, root-cause analysis, and discussion, thereby driving TCC.**
