# 🔤 Step1 - Attach

## Attach (Ownership & Opportunity Engagement)

**Goal:** Establish your presence early, build technical trust, and establish clear ownership\n**Responsibilities:**

* Join early-stage sales conversations with Sales reps
* Understand initial deal context and challenges
* Start thinking about technical success metrics
* Begin relationship-building with customer stakeholders


This phase formally links a Sales Engineer (SE) to your CRM's opportunity, establishing clear ownership and accountability. When the Primary SE, specialist SEs, overlays, and SMEs are "**ATTACH**ed" to an opportunity, it clarifies roles and responsibilities and enables a measurable **attach rate** KPI. This utilization KPI helps leaders track how effectively technical resources are deployed across all open opportunities. 

By **attaching** yourself and the necessary team members in the CRM, you create visibility into who guides the deal. It reinforces a culture where SEs are empowered to take charge of key accounts, ensuring that customer needs are adequately addressed from the earliest stage. This systematic approach also aids in resource planning and forecasting, as leadership can more accurately gauge which accounts have the right technical coverage and which might need additional support.
